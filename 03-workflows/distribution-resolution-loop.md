# Distribution ↔ Resolution Loop

This workflow connects the two transcript-derived digital models as one governed ANAS OS feedback system.

## Purpose

Use Distribution to increase useful exposure, feedback, trust signals, opportunities, and talent discovery. Use Resolution to turn those signals into better product, market, team, technology, economics, and operations decisions.

## Canonical loop

```text
Frame project uncertainty
        ↓
Resolution state + department map
        ↓
Choose R&D fronts + bounded vertical slice
        ↓
Prototype / pilot / content / release candidate
        ↓
Distribution
        ↓
Reach + repeat exposure + feedback + qualified inbound + opportunities
        ↓
Evidence recorded
        ↓
Resolution state updated
        ↓
Redraw / continue / increase capacity
        ↓
Next output
        ↺
```

## Operating rules

1. Distribution is not a vanity-metric system; optimize for useful surface area and qualified outcomes.
2. Resolution is not a planning-theatre system; every meaningful uncertainty should have an evidence path.
3. Keep market, product, team, technology, economics, distribution, and operations visible as separate resolution dimensions.
4. Use R&D only where the method is genuinely uncertain; timebox it and set a cost ceiling.
5. Prefer reversible delivery paths while uncertainty is high.
6. Do not scale headcount, technology spend, or distribution spend merely because funding or enthusiasm exists.
7. Treat attribution as uncertain unless evidence supports causality.
8. Human authority, ANAS gates, security policy, and financial controls remain sovereign over this workflow.
9. Transcript-derived numeric thresholds are defaults/hypotheses and must be validated before becoming operating policy.
10. Record the resulting learning so validated resolution can become a reusable playbook.

## Inputs

- project objective and constraints
- current resolution state
- department/output map
- experiments and vertical slices
- distribution/content results
- customer/market feedback
- capacity and economic assumptions
- applicable ANAS policy, authority, and release gates

## Outputs

- updated resolution state
- distribution scorecard and experiments
- decision/evidence log
- team and delivery recommendations
- explicit redraw/continue/kill decision
- reusable playbook when the project shape becomes repeatable

## Approval boundary

External publication, customer-impacting changes, consequential spending, credential changes, production release, destructive actions, and other governed mutations must pass the applicable ANAS authority and approval gates. This workflow never grants authority by itself.
