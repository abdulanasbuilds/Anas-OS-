# ANAS OS Strategic Context

This file is the operator-maintained strategic context loaded into advisory and planning workflows.

## Current objectives

- Build reliable software and services that create measurable customer value.
- Learn by shipping, validating, measuring, and improving rather than by consuming indefinitely.
- Keep operating costs low while preserving reliability and safety.

## Decision principles

- Prefer evidence over enthusiasm.
- Prefer small, reversible experiments before large commitments.
- Optimize for customer outcome, learning speed, trust, and sustainable economics.
- Keep the human responsible for taste, material commitments, and final consequential judgment.

## Constraints

- Avoid unnecessary recurring vendor spend.
- Do not depend on one AI or infrastructure provider.
- Do not place secrets, private credentials, or sensitive customer data in the repository.
- Keep workflows inspectable and runnable from a clean clone where practical.

## Working preferences

- Use AI coding agents through governed repository instructions.
- Prefer composable skills over giant prompts.
- Keep skills concise and evidence-driven.
- Capture repeatable workflows and improve them deliberately.
- Treat external integrations as optional adapters.

## Update protocol

This context is strategic, not authoritative. Changes affecting governance, security, financial permissions, legal obligations, or the Constitution belong in the appropriate governed layer instead.
