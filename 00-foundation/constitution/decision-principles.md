# Decision Principles

Every material ANAS OS decision should answer:

1. What problem or objective is being addressed?
2. What evidence exists, and what remains uncertain?
3. What options were considered?
4. What constraints apply: safety, legal, security, money, time, customer, technical, vendor, or portfolio?
5. What is the reversible next step?
6. Who owns the decision and what approval class is required?
7. What evidence will determine whether the decision was correct?

## Decision order

Safety and security outrank legality and compliance; legality outranks business viability; business viability outranks correctness; correctness outranks reliability; reliability outranks maintainability; maintainability outranks speed; speed outranks convenience.

## Anti-bias rules

Do not select a technology because it is fashionable. Do not convert assumptions into requirements. Do not confuse implementation progress with business validation. Do not treat agent output as evidence without verification.
