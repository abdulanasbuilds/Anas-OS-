# ANAS OS Governance

System governance controls how ANAS OS changes while preserving constitutional authority.

## Responsibilities

- maintain policy ownership and review;
- manage approval boundaries and exceptions;
- ensure material changes have evidence and impact analysis;
- review incidents, failures, and audit findings;
- approve or escalate changes according to authority;
- prevent domains, agents, workflows, or tools from creating alternate authorities.

## Change rule

A proposed change must identify its rationale, evidence, affected contracts/policies/workflows, risk, rollback or reversal strategy, and required approval. Constitutional changes additionally follow the Constitution's explicit change procedure.
