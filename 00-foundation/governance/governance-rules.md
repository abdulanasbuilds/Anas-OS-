# Governance Rules

Governance protects the integrity of ANAS OS while allowing practical execution. All material changes identify owner, rationale, impact, risk, evidence, approval requirement, rollback/compensation path, and verification method.

Exceptions are explicit, scoped, time-bound, owned, and auditable. An exception cannot weaken a constitutional prohibition or authorize a forbidden action.

Subsystems may propose policy, workflow, agent, template, or knowledge changes; adoption occurs through the change-control process.
