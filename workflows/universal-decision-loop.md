# Universal Decision & Execution Workflow

Use this workflow for meaningful work. Scale the depth to the risk and complexity.

## 1. Context
What is true now? What project, customer, environment, and constraints are relevant?

## 2. Objective
What measurable outcome are we trying to achieve?

## 3. Constraints
What cannot change? Include budget, time, technical, legal, security, and operational constraints.

## 4. Options
List viable approaches, including the option to do nothing.

## 5. Evidence
Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and unknowns. Gather enough evidence to make a proportionate decision.

## 6. Decision
Choose an option and record the reasoning.

## 7. Approval
Determine whether the decision falls within autonomous authority or requires escalation.

## 8. Execution
Assign the work to the appropriate human, agent, workflow, or tool.

## 9. Verification
Check acceptance criteria, security, correctness, and relevant non-functional requirements.

## 10. Measurement
Measure the outcome, not merely activity.

## 11. Feedback
Capture user, system, financial, operational, or technical feedback.

## 12. Learning
Record what should be retained, rejected, or investigated.

## 13. System update
Update project state, knowledge, policies, templates, workflows, or agent instructions only when justified by evidence.

## Fast-path rule

Low-risk reversible work may collapse multiple stages. High-risk, irreversible, customer-facing, security-sensitive, financial, or production work must preserve explicit decision, approval, execution, and verification evidence.
