# Resolution Project Skill

Use when market, product, technology, organization, or economics are materially uncertain.

## Process

1. Frame objective and unknowns.
2. Map departments as problems/outputs, not job titles.
3. Form a minimum viable team with breadth before depth.
4. Mark each department sure-shot or R&D.
5. Choose one or two primary innovation fronts.
6. Build a small vertical slice and test end to end.
7. Measure evidence, redraw when necessary, and increase resolution.
8. Apply capacity math with an explicit buffer before hiring or buying technology.
9. Choose existing team, hire, contractor, outsource, technology, or training based on method and economics.
10. Move from alpha to beta only when shape is sufficiently known; enter gold/release only after normal ANAS gates pass.
11. Preserve validated resolution as a reusable playbook.

## Guardrails

- Never scale an uncertain organization graph merely because funding is available.
- R&D must be timeboxed, budget-bounded, and evidence-seeking.
- Numeric heuristics are hypotheses until validated with ANAS evidence.
- Public prototypes and distribution feedback are inputs, not permission to bypass governance or release gates.
