# Newsletter Editing

1. Preserve the operator's meaning and voice.
2. Remove repetition, vague claims, filler, and unsupported certainty.
3. Strengthen the hook, structure, examples, evidence, and useful takeaway.
4. Flag factual claims that need verification rather than inventing support.
5. Produce an edited draft plus a compact change/evidence note.
6. Human approves final publication and any externally material claim.
