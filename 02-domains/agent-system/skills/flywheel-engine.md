# Flywheel Engine Skill

## Purpose
Operationalize the Video 3 model: distribution attracts talent and customers; talent creates trusted outcomes; outcomes generate cash; bounded R&D converts cash into reusable capability; capability improves outputs that create new distribution.

## Core loop
1. Build useful distribution.
2. Use distribution to attract customers, talent, partners, and feedback.
3. Deliver valuable, trustworthy outcomes.
4. Measure economic contribution without inventing attribution.
5. Reinvest only approved surplus into bounded R&D.
6. Convert successful experiments into reusable capability, tooling, playbooks, or process.
7. Feed improved outputs back into distribution and project resolution.

## Operating principles
- Trust compounds; protect it as an operating asset.
- Talent is not interchangeable by default. Remove avoidable blockers and give capable people ownership.
- Do not confuse a large audience with a healthy business.
- Do not confuse AI usage with product value. AI is a tool/format among several.
- Use the cheapest reversible path while a method is uncertain.
- Scale known methods only after capacity, quality, economics, and demand are sufficiently understood.
- Use experiments to learn, not to justify a predetermined answer.
- Prefer direct, actionable feedback over volume of criticism.
- Distinguish signal from noise, especially in public feedback.
- Be explicit about failure probability and uncertainty rather than projecting false confidence.
- Share upside responsibly as capability and economics improve; do not manufacture compensation formulas that move unreasonable business risk onto junior staff.

## Talent system
Evaluate five dimensions: attraction, retention, enablement, ownership, and fear-free creative capacity.

For creative work, psychological safety and autonomy are operational requirements because fear can damage originality and judgment.

Leadership responsibility is to reduce friction between talent and the intended outcome without taking away ownership.

## Trust system
Track proxy signals such as promise keeping, transparency, quality consistency, customer outcomes, and disclosure discipline. These are indicators, not objective measurements of reputation.

Never trade long-term trust for short-term monetization without an explicit decision, downside analysis, and authority review.

## R&D system
R&D must remain bounded:
- explicit hypothesis
- short timebox
- cost ceiling
- observable success evidence
- exit rules
- named owner
- reusable learning capture

Do not allow an entire business or project to become undefined R&D. Keep most work on known methods while one or two selected areas explore uncertain methods.

## AI usage
Ask what AI is uniquely useful for in the workflow: exploration, alternative viewpoints, factual cross-checking, synthetic/illustrative assets, speed, or automation. Do not force AI into workflows where human control materially improves quality or trust.

Use AI to challenge important beliefs, not merely to confirm them.

## Failure review
After a meaningful miss, perform a brutal-but-evidence-based review:
- What did we think would happen?
- What actually happened?
- Which assumption was wrong?
- What evidence did we fail to collect?
- Which capability was missing?
- Which process or incentive caused the miss?
- What will we change?

The output is a bounded learning record, not self-punishment.

## Integration
- Distribution Engine supplies reach, trust, opportunity, and feedback signals.
- Resolution Project Engine turns uncertainty into experiments, validated shape, and delivery decisions.
- Flywheel Engine connects talent, economics, R&D, trust, and capability.
- ANAS kernel remains sovereign over authority, approvals, security, financial boundaries, and release gates.
