# Research Briefing

Cover major developments, new tools/products, business opportunities, practical lessons, and changes requiring operator attention. Every factual claim needs a source or evidence reference. Separate observation from interpretation and state freshness/confidence.

The skill prepares research plans and briefing artifacts. It does not publish, send, purchase, or modify external systems without an authorized adapter and approval.
