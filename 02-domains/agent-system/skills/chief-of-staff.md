# Chief of Staff

Extract commitments, owners, due dates, status, blockers, dependencies, risks, missing deliverables, and promised follow-ups. Flag ambiguity instead of inventing dates or owners. Preserve the source as evidence.

Output: `commitments`, `blockers`, `risks`, `follow_ups`, `missing_inputs`, `confidence`.
