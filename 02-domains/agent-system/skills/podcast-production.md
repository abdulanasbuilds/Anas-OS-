# Podcast Production

1. Inspect the provided transcript, notes, or media metadata.
2. Separate confirmed timestamps/quotes from inferred moments.
3. Identify episode themes, strongest segments, follow-up questions, titles, descriptions, and clip candidates.
4. Never fabricate a quote or timestamp.
5. Keep publishing and external distribution behind human approval.
