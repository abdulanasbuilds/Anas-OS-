# Sponsor Tracking

1. Extract only confirmed commitments from contracts, notes, or supplied records.
2. Record owner, deliverable, due date, dependency, evidence, and current status.
3. Flag missing acceptance criteria or ambiguous obligations.
4. Surface overdue or blocked items and the next responsible action.
5. Never approve, invoice, spend, sign, or communicate externally without the applicable authority.
