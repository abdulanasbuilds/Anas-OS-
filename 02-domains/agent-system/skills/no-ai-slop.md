# No AI Slop

1. Remove generic filler, empty claims, repetitive framing, and unnecessary headings.
2. Replace vague advice with concrete actions, examples, evidence, or explicit uncertainty.
3. Preserve the operator's distinctive voice rather than optimizing for generic polish.
4. Check that every claim is supported, qualified, or clearly labeled as a hypothesis.
5. Prefer fewer stronger points over volume.
6. Require human review for final externally visible creative work.
