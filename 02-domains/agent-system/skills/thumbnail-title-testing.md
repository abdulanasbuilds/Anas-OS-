# Thumbnail & Title Testing

1. Define audience, promise, topic, and channel context.
2. Generate materially different hypotheses rather than cosmetic variants.
3. Score clarity, curiosity, credibility, specificity, and promise-match.
4. Use measured historical performance when available; label guesses as hypotheses.
5. Keep final creative selection human-controlled and record the winning evidence.
